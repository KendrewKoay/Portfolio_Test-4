I’m a Malaysian designer, educator and researcher specialising in graphic design, visual communication, design education and digital creativity. 我是来自马来西亚的设计师、教育者与研究者，专注于平面设计、视觉传达、设计教育与数字创意。

My practice moves between the studio and the classroom — making things, teaching things, and asking why visual communication works the way it does. 我的实践游走于工作室与教室之间 —— 动手做、用心教，并不断追问视觉传达为何如此运作。
